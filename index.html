Mayor of Bad Town

A free daily comedy card game. You're the new mayor of the worst town in the county, and every night is a re-election.

Every day brings 20 ridiculous problems. Swipe yes or no. Keep the People, Business, the Law and the Press happy, and don't let the Treasury run dry. Your deputy is Randy, a raccoon. He is trying to help.

  • A new day of cards every day, and a new themed week: Goose Migration, Heatwave, Festival Week and The Big Freeze.
  • Over 100 funny endings to collect in your Scrapbook, from being out-voted by a sandwich to signing the town away on a napkin.
  • Secret characters you can only meet with the right chain of decisions.
  • After Hours: riddles that pass laws, late-night visitors and sneak missions.

Free. No downloads. No sign-up. Plays on your phone or computer.
